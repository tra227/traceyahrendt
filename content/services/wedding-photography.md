## Your Day, Beautifully Told

Your wedding day is a once-in-a-lifetime celebration of your love story. My approach blends photojournalistic candids with timeless, editorial portraits — capturing the laughter, tears, stolen glances, and spontaneous moments that make your day uniquely yours.

## My Approach

I believe the best wedding photography happens when you forget the camera is there. While I'll guide you through portraits and key moments, much of the day is captured as it naturally unfolds. The result is a collection of images that feel real, emotional, and true to who you are as a couple.

## The Planning Process

Great wedding photography starts long before the first shutter click. We'll meet to walk through your timeline, discuss your vision, scout your venue, and plan for the moments that matter most to you. On your wedding day, you can relax knowing every detail is covered.

## Custom Packages

Every wedding is different, and your photography package should reflect that. From intimate elopements to grand celebrations, I'll build a custom package that fits your day.
