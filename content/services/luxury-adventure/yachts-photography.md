A yacht session can focus on the vessel itself, onboard lifestyle, or portraits in a maritime setting. We’ll discuss the brief, light, dock access, and whether photography takes place at berth or during an outing.

Tell me the vessel location, image goals, and planned setting. Owner permission, boarding arrangements, weather, and safe access are confirmed before the shoot.
