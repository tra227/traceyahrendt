## Those Tiny Details Don't Last

The curl of tiny fingers, the softness of new skin, that impossibly small yawn — these fleeting moments deserve to be preserved beautifully. Newborn sessions are gentle, patient, and always led by your baby's needs.

I recommend booking during pregnancy so we can plan everything in advance. Sessions are ideally held within the first 5-14 days when babies are sleepiest and most naturally curled.

## Safety First

Your baby's safety and comfort are my top priority. I'm trained in safe newborn posing techniques and never force a pose. Sessions move at your baby's pace — with plenty of time for feeding, soothing, and diaper changes.

## What to Expect

We'll capture a mix of solo baby portraits, family groupings, and detail shots (tiny toes, eyelashes, hands). Props, wraps, and blankets are provided, though you're welcome to bring meaningful items from home.
