## Your Session

Lifestyle sessions center on the moments that make your story yours: time at home, a favorite place, or an activity you enjoy together. Gentle direction helps create a cohesive collection while leaving room for spontaneity.

## Planning Together

Share the routines, places, and people you want to remember, along with how you hope to use your images.
