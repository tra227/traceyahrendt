## Your Love, Your Way

An engagement session is more than a photo op — it's a chance to celebrate this incredible moment in your relationship. Whether you want a casual, playful shoot or something more dramatic and romantic, we'll create images that feel authentically you.

## More Than Just Photos

Beyond beautiful images for save-the-dates and your wedding website, your engagement session is a dress rehearsal for your wedding day. You'll get comfortable in front of the camera, learn your best angles, and build a rapport with me that translates into more natural, relaxed wedding photos.

## Location Ideas

The best engagement photos happen in places that mean something to you — where you had your first date, your favorite park, the café where you always grab coffee together. I also know beautiful spots throughout Ft. Lauderdale and the surrounding area if you need inspiration.
