## Celebrate This Chapter

Pregnancy is one of the most transformative experiences of your life — a fleeting season that deserves to be documented beautifully. Maternity sessions capture the glow, the anticipation, and the deep connection between you and your growing family.

## When to Book

The ideal window for maternity photos is between 28-36 weeks, when your bump is beautifully round but you're still comfortable. I recommend reaching out during your second trimester so we can secure your preferred date and plan your session.

## Making It Personal

Whether you envision a sun-drenched outdoor session, an intimate at-home shoot, or elegant studio portraits, we'll design the session around your style. Partners, children, and even pets are welcome — this is about your family's story.
